Nimbus team handbook — fictional RAG demo

This document describes an invented company. It is sample data for testing document ingestion, retrieval, and written answers. It is not a real company policy.

Remote work
Nimbus staff may work remotely two days each week. The days must be agreed with their manager. Staff should update the shared team calendar before the start of the week.

Travel expenses
Travel expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the trip. Each claim must include an itemized receipt. The finance team reviews complete claims within five working days.

Lost equipment
If a work laptop is lost, notify the IT team within one hour. Include the device asset number and the approximate time and place it was last seen. Do not wait until the next working day to report it.

Learning sessions
The team holds a learning session on the first Friday of each month. Anyone can volunteer to demonstrate a tool or share a project. This handbook does not specify an annual training budget.
